What i do when the shop is closed

I close the shop in Costa Road at six and the rest of the evening is mine. These are some of the things I do when the shop is closed. I like to cook, watch movies, and walk with my family.

The shop sits just off the main road, so I can hear the traffic and the people walking by. I also like to walk to the beach with my sister, and we talk about our day and our plans for the future.

My top five beaches

This one is an ol beacause the order means something here. Number one is number one and i will argue with anyone about it. including my family and friends.

  1. Kartong.
  2. Sanyang.
  3. Kotu.
  4. Bijilo
  5. Cape Point in Bakau

Kartong is my favorite beach because it is quiet and peaceful. I like to go there with my sister and we talk about our day and our plans for the future.

Kotu is the onei actually go to the most because it is close to my house and I can walk there. I like to go there with my family and we play football and swim in the sea.
